Peak Galleria

Sector
Retail
Area
12,000+ sqm
Scope
Structural + facade + fit-out

Our founder, Tristan Koo, was part of the extensive renovation of Peak Galleria, one of Hong Kong’s most iconic landmarks. This high-profile project involved a complete transformation of the retail complex to enhance its functionality, aesthetic appeal, and visitor experience.

The scope of work included significant structural alterations to accommodate a new architectural layout and exterior stone and glazing facade, designed to modernize the building’s function and appearance while blending harmoniously with the surrounding natural and urban landscape.

Internally, we completed a full fit-out of the mall’s retail, communal and recreational areas, including flooring, lighting, ceilings, mechanical and electrical systems, landscaping and other key interior elements. The successful delivery of this high-profile project highlights our expertise in executing large-scale commercial renovations with precision, quality, and efficiency.
