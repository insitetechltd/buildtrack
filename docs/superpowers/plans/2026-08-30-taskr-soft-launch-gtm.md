## 3. Positioning lock

| Slot | Copy |
|---|---|
| **Category** | Construction **field evidence** app — not Procore, not DingTalk, not WhatsApp. |
| **Buyer** | 二判 / 裝修 / 執漏 **老闆** who can spend HK$160 without procurement. Influencer: 地盤總管 / 管工. Daily user: 師傅. |
| **Enemy** | The 40-person WhatsApp group where the proof photo died. |
| **Why now** | Concealment works, punch/snag, and defect claims are where photos already exist — they just are not assigned or owned. |
| **Price frame** | HK$160/mo ≈ less than one site tea round a week. Do **not** compare to Procore. |
| **Not for (yet)** | Main-con head office IT, architects needing CDE, anyone asking “can the consultant log in on their own company?” |
| **Tagline (locked)** | **Construction software by the industry.** / **建造業自己人做嘅軟件。** |

The word you want is not “constructors.” In HK/UK English that usually means a main contractor. Listing “designers, architects, builders, contractors” always drops someone (管工, 師傅, QS, 安全主任, 顧問…). The inclusive noun is **the industry** (建造業 / 業界). CIC’s formal word is **practitioners** (從業員) — correct, colder.

**Do not enumerate roles in the tagline.** The claim is *who made it* (people who work in construction), not “this app replaces every profession’s software.” Taskr is still the field loop. Architects are in the industry; they are not promised a drawing register.

| Slot | Copy |
|---|---|
| **Eyebrow** | CONSTRUCTION SOFTWARE BY THE INDUSTRY |
| **Tagline** | Construction software by the industry. |
| **zh-HK** | 建造業自己人做嘅軟件。 |
| **Support (once, not the logo)** | Made in construction — for the whole job, not one profession. / 業界做畀業界，唔係單一工種嘅工具。 |
| **H1 (unchanged)** | Capture the work. Assign it. Approve it. |

Alternates if you reject “the industry”: (2) Construction software by practitioners. (3) From the industry. For the job. (4) 業界做嘅建造軟件。 Skip “by builders / by contractors / by constructors.”

**ICP order (soft launch):**

1. Fit-out / A&A / 執漏 contractors (photo volume explodes at handover).  
2. Specialist subs with **concealment sign-off** pain: waterproofing, E&M, fire services, rebar pre-pour.  
3. One friendly **site agent** at a main-con project (lighthouse protocol, not a procurement deal).  
4. Warm network (founder fills names — §8).

Avoid as first 10: listed main contractors’ HQ, CITF-first buyers (“等批咗先買”), anyone who needs Android this month.
