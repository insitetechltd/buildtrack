## Founder draft (refine this; do not ignore the intent)

Taskr helps to record, organize and execute all the challenges in every construction project. From the owner, to the architect, to the consultants, to the contractors, Taskr makes sure every details, every changes, every specifications reaches to the relevant parties by ensuring accountability with complete audit trails on every issues recorded in our system. ## Context (product that actually ships)

- App name: **Taskr**. Company: **Insite Works Limited**. iOS only. Hong Kong, billed in **HKD**.

## Output format (mandatory)

1. **Verdict:** KEEP-INTENT / KEEP-INTENT-WITH-FIXES (always expect fixes)
2. **Findings table:** severity C/H/M/L — what in the founder draft is false, vague, or hard to parse
3. **Must-cut list** (max 5): phrases that cannot ship
4. **Recommended Description** — paste-ready, scannable, **≤1500 characters** if possible (App Store allows 4000; we want simple). Short sentences. Concrete verbs. One idea per sentence.
5. **Optional promotional text** ≤170 characters
6. **Character counts** for description and promo
7. **Delta vs founder draft** in 3 bullets (what we kept / dropped / sharpened)

Do not invent features. Prefer “who did what, with which photo, when” over “complete audit trails on every issues.”
