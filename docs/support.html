Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new task?
To create a new task, tap the "+" button on the main screen or navigate to the Tasks tab and select "New Task". Fill in the task details including title, description, assignee, and due date.
How do I upload photos to a task?
When viewing or editing a task, tap the camera or photo icon. You can either take a new photo or select an existing photo from your device's photo library. The app will request permission to access your camera and photo library the first time you use this feature.
How do I assign a task to a team member?
When creating or editing a task, use the "Assign To" field to select a team member from your project. You can search for team members by name or email address.
Can I use Taskr offline?
Taskr requires an internet connection to sync data with the server. However, you can view recently loaded tasks and make changes that will sync when your connection is restored.
How do I update my profile?
Navigate to your profile by tapping your profile icon or name. From there, you can update your name, email, phone number, and profile photo.
I'm having trouble logging in. What should I do?
Make sure you're using the correct email address and password. If you've forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen. If you continue to have issues, please contact support at support@insiteworks.com.
