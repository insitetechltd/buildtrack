Taskr is a construction field app from Insite (Insite Tech Ltd). Access is sold as an organizational (company) subscription. Seats are provisioned by the company admin; there is no public self-serve join of an arbitrary company.

Use of the app

  • Use Taskr only for work your company has authorized on that company’s projects.
  • Do not upload content you are not allowed to share (including photos of people or sites where you lack permission).
  • Keep your sign-in details confidential. Company admins manage who has a seat.

Data and accounts

Task, photo, and project records belong to the company that created them. A worker cannot wipe company data by leaving or by requesting account removal in the app. Seat removal is an admin action; jobsite history may keep actor names for auditability.

Availability

We provide Taskr as-is for this release candidate. Features, uptime, and support channels may change. We may suspend access that we reasonably believe is abusive or unauthorized.
