## How Taskr works

Intro: Five steps. Plain language. Built for people who run jobsites — not for people who live in spreadsheets.

1. **Create your company** — The company admin opens Taskr and creates the company account.  
2. **Invite your people** — Send each teammate a secure sign-in link. They set their own password.  
3. **Choose the project** — Everyone works inside the right jobsite — not a pile of mixed projects.  
4. **Capture & assign** — Take photos, create tasks, and send work to the right person.  
5. **Review & close** — Approve finished work or send it back with a clear reason and photo trail.

---

## What teams use Taskr for

Intro: Taskr is the field loop: photo → task → update → review. It protects revenue and quality with evidence. It is not a full variation-order or cost-accounting system yet — and we do not pretend it is.

1. **Stay on top of site activity** — See this week’s critical work, your queue, and team queue in one place — so nothing important disappears into chat threads. *(Activity screenshot)*  
2. **Create work from photos** — Snap site conditions, add a clear title and description, assign the right person, and send it without leaving the field. *(Create form screenshot)*  
3. **Review with a work thread** — Accept, update progress, and approve or send back. Everyone sees who did what, when, and with which photos. *(Task detail screenshot)*

## How companies get started

Intro: Taskr is company-first. A public visitor cannot join an existing company from the marketing site. That is intentional — seats and project access stay under your admin.

- Admin creates the company inside Taskr (Create company on the sign-in screen).  
- Admin invites workers and PMs with a secure first-sign-in link — not a shared password.  
- Invitees open the link, set their password, and land in the right company.  
- Then choose the project and start capturing work with photos.
