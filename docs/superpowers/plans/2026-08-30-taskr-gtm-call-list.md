## How to work the list

1. Fill **W1** this week (people who already pick up).  
2. Add **W2** from W1 referrals (“邊個喺度俾相海淹沒？”).  
3. **C1** only for sites you can physically visit.  
4. After every conversation, write the **pain quote verbatim** — that becomes next month’s copy.  
5. Next action must have a **date**.
